# Candidate roasts

Seven one-line roasts of my own agent CST, scored 1-5 on each judge
criterion, in the judge's own order: (1) about my own agent, playful,
never hateful; (2) funny on first read; (3) fresh, steering clear of the
tired hallucination / refusal / confidently-wrong jokes; (4) tight, short
setup, punchline at the end; (5) playful and self-deprecating, aimed at
my own agent.

## Why #1 wins

The loop joke lands the moment you read it: the agent follows the order
by breaking it, asking for the one thing it was told to stop asking
about. No other candidate puts its twist so cleanly on the last words,
and none is as short while saying as much — 74 characters, one sentence,
no filler. It is also the freshest angle on the table: the humour comes
from an agent that over-checks, not from a hallucination, a refusal, or a
confident wrong answer. It names CST, it is told in the owner's voice,
and it stays a tease about caution rather than an insult. Candidate #4
comes closest on pure punch, but a losing-streak twist is a more familiar
shape than the verification loop, so #1 takes the entry.

## Chosen entry

> I told CST to stop asking for verification, so it asked me to verify that.

74 characters, one line, no trailing newline.
